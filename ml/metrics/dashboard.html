Ranking & calibration

AUC 0.958 and average precision 0.952 say the model ranks matchups far better than the 88.3% accuracy alone suggests. The calibration curve and Brier score (0.081) say its probabilities are trustworthy — which matters because the app shows a confidence number. Note the probabilities take only 18 distinct values: an honest artifact of having just three vehicles.

Why it predicts what it does

The coefficients look wrong until you check them: drag (cda_diff) gets a positive weight, implying more drag helps — physically backwards. The correlation heatmap and permutation importance explain it.

Permutation importance

Finding — collinearity, not a bug. COLLINEARITY EXPLAINS THE FLIP (SUPPORTED). The cda_diff coefficient is positive (+0.478 std), which is physically backwards -- more drag should hurt. But with only 3 cars, drag is nearly collinear with power (r=0.96) and risk (r=0.94); power and risk correlate at r=1.00. The genuinely predictive, physically-correct signals (power+, risk+) load onto these correlated axes; L2 regularization (C=0.1) then splits the shared weight and lets drag absorb a positive sign it does not causally deserve. Permutation importance ranks features by real predictive contribution as risk_diff > power_diff_kw > hov_eligible_diff > mass_diff_kg > cda_diff_m2, which does not mirror the distorted raw-coefficient magnitudes -- the signature of collinearity.
